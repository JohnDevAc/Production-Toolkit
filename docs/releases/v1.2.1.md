Production Toolkit 1.2.1 checks for updates only at startup or when you click **Check for updates**.

- Removed the automatic check after a managed application's installer closes.
- Installed versions, app icons and card colours now refresh only during startup or an explicit update check. Click **Check for updates** or reopen the toolkit after installing an app to refresh its card.
- There are no scheduled checks, background polling, or checks when an app launches, a channel changes or focus returns.

Run **Production-Toolkit-1.2.1-win-x64-Setup.exe**, or accept the toolkit's update prompt on its next startup or update check. Setup replaces the existing installation, preserves settings and shortcuts, and relaunches an open toolkit.
